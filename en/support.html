Help with progress, audio and advertising

Export a backup in My town before uninstalling. To restore, choose Import progress backup. An invalid file does not replace your data.

For silent speech, install a Japanese voice in iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content. Microphone permission is optional. Recording never produces a pronunciation score.

No ad? Continue learning for free. For advertising feedback, include the app version, approximate time and ad format. Do not send sensitive data or recordings.
