Nihongo Quest: Japanese Town

Learn everyday Japanese in small quests

Start with 30 lessons in a convenience store, station and school. Read, listen, recall and arrange Japanese. Teaching language: English, 简体中文, Tiếng Việt or Bahasa Indonesia.

Lessons and progress work offline. System Japanese voices must be installed for offline audio. Ads need internet and may be unavailable.

Unlock every lesson by passing the previous lesson in the same scene with 80% independent accuracy. Quick unlock is optional: an earned ad reward opens your selected lesson and adds 10 red flowers. When all lessons are open, you can still choose a rewarded ad for 10 flowers.

Flowers buy garden decorations and growth. They have no cash value. No account or subscription is required.

Beginner supplementary practice; not a complete JLPT course or a guarantee of examination results. System speech; no claim of native human review.
